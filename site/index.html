04 — THE RECEIPTS

Show the work.

Each election-winner event is one race. Named candidate “Yes” prices are normalized to 100% within that race; the highest-priced candidate is the favorite. The score uses the multiclass Brier formula, so a confident wrong call receives a larger penalty.

Historical prices can be aggregated or stale. We reject observations more than 24 hours old and retain every price’s timestamp in the downloadable data. This 2025 edition is a historical pipeline test of tagged U.S. race-winner markets; it is not a claim about all Polymarket election bets.

THE FORMULA
Brier = ½ Σ(p − y)²

For each candidate, p is its normalized forecast probability; y is 1 for the winner and 0 otherwise. We average race scores at each cutoff.
