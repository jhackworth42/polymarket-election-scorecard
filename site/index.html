05 / METHODS & SOURCES

What counts—and what doesn’t

We score one direct election-winner event per race. Named, traded candidate “Yes” prices are normalized to sum to 100% within that event. A favorite “hit” means the highest-priced candidate won. A race enters a cutoff score only when its eventual winner has a usable historical price.

The Brier score is ½ × Σ(probability − outcome)² across candidates; lower is better and 0 is perfect. We exclude prices more than 24 hours older than a cutoff and retain each observation time. The 2025 edition is a historical test of tagged U.S. markets, not every election or every Polymarket bet.
