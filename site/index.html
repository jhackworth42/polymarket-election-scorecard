METHODS & SOURCES

How this is counted

One Polymarket race-winner event counts as one race. Traded candidate and party “Yes” prices are normalized to sum to 100% within the event. The favorite is the highest-priced outcome at a checkpoint. Brier is ½ × the sum of squared differences between every outcome's price and the result. The six-hour cutoff is noon ET, six hours before the first U.S. poll closing. Only verified Polymarket resolutions enter headline accuracy, misses and calibration; unsettled races remain pending.
