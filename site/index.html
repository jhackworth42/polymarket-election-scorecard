METHODS & SOURCES

What this page measures

We froze a set of 2026 Senate, governor and House race-winner markets before outcomes were known. Each race appears once in the movement ranking. Candidate and party outcomes are used for the starting-confidence buckets. The two snapshots are fixed in Eastern time; prices after 5 p.m. ET are excluded from this comparison. Observed timestamps, market selection decisions and code are public.
