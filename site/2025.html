06 / METHODS & SOURCES

How this is scored

One direct election-winner event counts as one race. Named, traded candidate “Yes” prices are normalized to sum to 100% within that event. A favorite “hit” means the highest-priced candidate won. Brier score is ½ × Σ(probability − outcome)² across candidates; lower is better.

Time is measured in days before each race’s election date, at 15:00 UTC. “Contested” is fixed using the seven-day forecast: no candidate at 80% or higher. The time chart uses the same races with both 30-day and one-day prices. We reject prices more than 24 hours older than a cutoff, and we stop at election morning to avoid mixing forecasts with emerging results. The 2025 edition is a historical test of tagged U.S. markets, not every election or every Polymarket bet.
